It's **#MiddleGradeMonday**, so let me explain: middle grade books are generally
written for readers between about 7–8 and 12 — though that's approximate, like
any age I'll give you for a book. So many of us over 12 love middle grade too,
because they tackle real themes you can relate to at any age, and you can read
them as a family. There's every kind, including great starting points for
conversations about topics adults sometimes know less about than young people
do (and sometimes the other way around).

**Los Explora Gómez** is a middle-grade series that even under-8s would enjoy
reading along with — and it's great fun. A "light" summer read for any moment,
with a hilarious but adventurous, competitive family that makes you want to
travel by camper and, obviously, to **explore**, to work as a team, and to have
a brilliant time.

Best of all, this is the first in a saga, and we couldn't be happier about it.

_Written by Teresa Blanch · Published by Montena (Penguin Kids España)._
