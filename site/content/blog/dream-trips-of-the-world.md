What is your summer dream trip? Are you one of those who escapes the heat, or one
of those who chases it?

We used to be a coast people escaped the heat *to*, and chased good food for —
but in the middle of this heatwave, we're hiding and escaping too! The sea always
helps here, though. You won't catch me out of the water, especially not in the
sun, unless I'm doing something.

**Dream Trips of the World** is one of those Lonely Planet books I love to browse
through over and over, because even if they aren't all my dream trips — or if
they include some I've already been on — they always get me dreaming of escaping.
Or is it called travel?
