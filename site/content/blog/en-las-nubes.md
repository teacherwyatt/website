I'd been trying for weeks to find the right words for a book this special — one
that made us feel so *seen*. Being a bit of a dreamer myself, I kept putting it
off, until a cloud that looked like an open book with its pages in the wind
finally inspired me.

**Dani** (Bobby in the original) is a story in himself. It takes the main
character a while to realise that he is the way he is, and that he can't always
be up in the clouds, nor always with his feet on the ground. With a wonderful
circle of support — two friends, his parents, and a teacher who learns a lot in
this story too (because we teachers learn as much as the little ones) — Dani
finds his balance in the world, and his way not just to grow his creativity, but
to **inspire**.

It's a book I'd recommend in any classroom, including the outdoor one, the one
that heads out into the open air. Because "the classroom" is the people in it.
Shall we take this story to the beach?

_Published in Spanish by NubeOcho._
