Oh no, teacher Kris! I mean… **Lottie, Lottie Brooks!**

Although most of my students learn English, they also borrow books from the
**multilingual library** we keep at The Wyatt House — and this summer we're still
open, with reading for every taste. This double treat is translated from an
English hit, _The Extremely Embarrassing Life of Lottie Brooks_, and it reminds
me of the diary of Adrian Mole (and yes, maybe the one you simply call Greg) —
but Lottie is *so* much fun, and these girls recommend it even more for the
summer and beyond.

Lottie is 11 and three-quarters, and although she writes in lowercase print, she
also draws lots of pictures with speech bubbles — the text kind, not the cheese
kind — so you can take it camping or to the beach just the same.

Happy reading!
