Hi, I'm **Kristin** — the teacher behind TeacherWyatt.

I teach English and Spanish to people of all ages here in **Nigrán, Galicia**,
and online to students anywhere. My learners come from all sorts of language
backgrounds — English, Spanish, French, and Italian speakers — and that mix is
one of my favourite things about the job.

I believe learning a language should feel warm and human, not like a test. So my
lessons are relaxed and full of real conversation, and we use **stories and
books** a lot — especially with younger learners. There's no faster way to fall
in love with a language than a good book you can't put down.

Whether you're a parent looking for lessons for your child, an adult brushing up
for work or travel, or someone just starting out, we'll go at your pace and make
it enjoyable.

The first lesson is always **free**, so we can meet, see how we get on, and plan
what's right for you.
